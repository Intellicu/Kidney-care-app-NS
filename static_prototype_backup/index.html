How to test

Use first morning urine when you can. Read the colour only after the wait time.

1
Wash your hands About 30 sec
Wash with soap and water, then dry. Keep the strip bottle closed until you are ready.
2
Collect midstream urine Sample
Start urinating, then catch the middle of the stream in a clean cup. First morning urine is best.
3
Dip the strip 2 sec
Dip the pads for 1 to 2 seconds only. Do not leave the strip sitting in the cup.
4
Wait, then place the strip 60 sec
Tap off extra drops. Lay the strip flat on the reading card under the holder.
