# Badge

A small pill for status, source or classification beside a title.

Source: `src/components/ui/badge.jsx`.

- Variants: `default` (`primary`), `secondary`, `outline`, `destructive`.
- Use for guideline source ("KDIGO 2024"), phenotype or availability ("Offline ready"). Keep to one or two words.
- For clinical severity use `SeverityBadge` instead.
- The consumer supplies the text as children.
