# Dialog

A Radix modal for confirmations and focused tasks.

Source: `src/components/ui/dialog.jsx`.

- Parts: `Dialog`, `DialogTrigger`, `DialogContent`, `DialogHeader`, `DialogTitle`, `DialogDescription`, `DialogFooter`, `DialogClose`.
- Use to confirm an order or a high-risk action; the title is the question ("Start IV methylprednisolone?") and the description gives the exact dose.
- Footer: a `outline` cancel followed by one `default` confirm.
